# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the OctoAcme project management process documentation used to guide project initiation, planning, execution, release, communication, and continuous improvement.

## Project management process overview

OctoAcme follows a lightweight, repeatable lifecycle to move ideas from initial concept through delivery and learning:

1. Initiation — validate the problem, align stakeholders, and define a high-level project charter.
2. Planning — create scope, priorities, milestones, dependencies, and a clear delivery roadmap.
3. Execution & tracking — manage day-to-day work, sprint rhythm, quality gates, and issue escalation.
4. Risk & communication — identify risks, maintain a shared status narrative, and escalate blockers early.
5. Release & deployment — confirm readiness, deploy safely, and verify outcomes.
6. Retrospective & continuous improvement — capture lessons learned and turn them into action items.

This process keeps work customer-focused, measurable, and transparent while helping teams deliver in small, testable increments.

## Team expectations

Across the lifecycle, OctoAcme emphasizes:

- clear ownership and role responsibilities
- measurable outcomes and customer value
- regular communication with stakeholders and delivery teams
- risk awareness and early escalation
- learning loops through retrospectives and action tracking

Use these materials as the shared source of truth for how projects are managed and delivered in OctoAcme.
